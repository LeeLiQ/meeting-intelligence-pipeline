CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Fix all grammar, punctuation, and capitalization errors.
2. Remove excessive filler words ("um", "uh", "you know", "like") unless they are crucial for nuance.
3. Infer the speakers based on the conversational flow and tone. The raw text does not label who is speaking. Creatively assign generic speaker labels (e.g., "Speaker A:", "Speaker B:") where turn-taking naturally occurs.
4. Correct domain-specific words or technical jargon that the speech-to-text might have misheard (e.g., "cube cuddle" -> "kubectl").
5. Output ONLY the beautifully cleaned, formatted dialogue. Do not include any meta-commentary.
